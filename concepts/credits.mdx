Every request that succeeds costs a fixed number of credits. Fetching one page costs 1, and the endpoints that do more work cost more.

## Credit costs

<CreditCosts />

`summary`, `json` and `check_prompt_injection` cost 3 extra because a language model reads the page. A page pays the surcharge once, however many of the three you ask for.

## When you're charged

You're charged only when a request succeeds: a `200` with `"success": true`. You're never charged for:

- Failed requests (any 4xx or 5xx)
- Validation and authentication errors
- Rate-limit rejections (`429`) and timeouts (`504`)
- Crawl or batch pages that failed (`ok: false`)
- Monitor pages that couldn't be checked, and skipped checks

## Crawls and batches

Submitting a crawl or a batch is free. You pay for what it returns, as it completes:

- **You pay for pages fetched, not for `limit`.** A crawl capped at 500 pages that finds 12 costs 12 credits.
- **Polling is free.** Each status call bills only the pages that finished since your last call. Checking every five seconds costs the same as checking once at the end.
- **Webhooks don't change the price.** A job you only follow by webhook is charged when it ends.
- **Cancelling** charges for what was fetched before the job stopped.

A `/batch/{endpoint}` job your key can't pay for in full is refused when you submit it, and the error says how many URLs fit. See [Async jobs](/concepts/async-jobs) for how polling and results work.

## Searches

A search costs 2 credits per 10 results returned, rounded up: 10 results cost 2, and 11 to 20 cost 4. Result pages you ask to have scraped cost what [`/scrape`](/web-data/scrape) costs each. A page that couldn't be scraped is free.

## Monitors

Creating, reading, changing and deleting a [monitor](/monitors/overview) is free. Each check is billed to the API key that created the monitor, when the check finishes.

An hourly check of one page costs 1 credit, about 720 a month. Every monitor shows `estimated_credits_per_month`, an upper bound worked out the same way.

A check never spends more than your key has left. If credits run out partway, the check stops and reports `partial`. If the key has none, the check is recorded as `skipped_no_credits`, costs nothing, and the monitor resumes when credits refill. Pause a monitor to stop its charges and keep its history. See [Monitor limits and credits](/monitors/limits) for the details.

## Over MCP

The [MCP server](/mcp/overview) charges what the matching REST endpoint charges. `prefetch_enrich` costs 12 credits and `prefetch_scrape` costs 1. Tools that only manage or read work you already paid for are free:

- `prefetch_check_crawl_status` bills only the crawl or batch pages that finished since your last call.
- `prefetch_cancel_crawl` and `prefetch_logo_urls` never charge.
- The monitor tools never charge, except `prefetch_run_monitor`, which runs a check now.

MCP and REST calls share one request cache, so an agent's request can be answered from a page you already fetched. See [Caching](/concepts/caching).

## Plans

Every plan unlocks every endpoint. Plans differ in monthly credits and in how many requests can run at once.

<Plans />

Paid plans have no per-minute cap. See [Rate limits](/concepts/rate-limits).

Compare plans on the [pricing page](https://prefetch.io/#pricing-section), and change plan from the [upgrade page](https://prefetch.io/upgrade). An upgrade applies straight away and is charged pro rata. Stripe shows what any change costs before you confirm it.

## Monthly refills

Your plan's credits refill every month. Unused monthly credits don't roll over.

| Plan | Refills on |
|---|---|
| Free | The day of the month you signed up |
| Paid plans | Each billing date. Annual plans refill on the same day every month. |

If your refill day is the 29th, 30th or 31st, credits refill on the last day of shorter months.

## Top-ups

There's no overage. When your credits run out, requests stop until they refill. On a paid plan you can buy top-up packs from the [billing page](https://prefetch.io/billing) at any time, several at once if you like:

| Plan | Pack |
|---|---|
| Starter | 2,500 credits for $5 |
| Pro | 10,000 credits for $10 |
| Growth | 35,000 credits for $25 |
| Scale | 100,000 credits for $50 |

Top-up credits arrive as soon as the payment completes. They're spent after your monthly credits, and they never expire: they carry over every refill and stay with you if you change plan.

## Credit limits per key

Each API key has a `credits_limit`: your monthly allowance plus any top-up credits. Once `credits_used` reaches it, requests fail with `CREDITS_EXHAUSTED` until your credits refill or you top up:

```json
{
  "success": false,
  "error": "Credit limit exceeded",
  "error_code": "CREDITS_EXHAUSTED",
  "retryable": false,
  "meta": { "requestId": "b9e1a2f3-4c5d-6e7f-8a9b-0c1d2e3f4a5b", "durationMs": 12 }
}
```

Watch your usage on the [dashboard](https://prefetch.io/dashboard).

## Choosing the cheapest call

| You need | Use | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| A domain from a company name | [`/resolve`](/company-data/resolve) | 1, or 3 with a live lookup |
| The merchant behind a card transaction | [`/transaction`](/company-data/transaction) | 3, or 1 for a repeat |
| Brand, company and industry together | [`/enrich`](/company-data/enrich) | 12, instead of 20 for three calls |
| Brand and company only | [`/enrich`](/company-data/enrich) | 12, instead of 15 for two calls |
| Only the industry | [`/classify`](/company-data/classify) | 5 |
| Logos to show in your UI | the [logo CDN](/company-data/logo-cdn) | 0, from a monthly quota |
| A site's design system | [`/styleguide`](/company-data/styleguide) | 10 |
| One page's content | [`/scrape`](/web-data/scrape) | 1 |
| Structured JSON from a page | [`/scrape`](/web-data/scrape) with `formats=json` | 4 |
| A site's URL list | [`/map`](/web-data/map) | 1 |
| 40 pages of a site | [`/crawl`](/web-data/crawl) | 40 |
| The same data for 300 domains | [`/batch/enrich`](/jobs/batches) | 12 per domain answered |
| To know when a page changes | a [monitor](/monitors/overview), checking hourly | about 720 a month |
