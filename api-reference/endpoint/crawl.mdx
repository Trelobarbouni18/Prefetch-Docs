---
title: "POST /crawl"
sidebarTitle: "Start a crawl"
description: "Crawl a site, or a section of it, and get every page as markdown. Free to submit, then 1 credit per page."
openapi: "POST /crawl"
---

Starts a crawl and returns `202` with a crawl id. Read the pages with [`GET /crawl/{id}`](/api-reference/endpoint/crawl-status).

**Free to submit.** 1 credit per page returned, 4 with `summary`, `json` or `check_prompt_injection`. Failed requests are never charged.
