---
title: "POST /batch/scrape"
sidebarTitle: "Start a batch scrape"
description: "Scrape up to 500 URLs as one job. Free to submit, then 1 credit per page."
openapi: "POST /batch/scrape"
---

Starts a batch scrape and returns `202` with a job id. Read the pages with [`GET /batch/scrape/{id}`](/api-reference/endpoint/batch-scrape-status).

**Free to submit.** 1 credit per page returned, 4 with `summary`, `json` or `check_prompt_injection`. Failed requests are never charged.
