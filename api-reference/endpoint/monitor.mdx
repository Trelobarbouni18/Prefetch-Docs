title: "POST /monitor"
sidebarTitle: "Create a monitor"
description: "Watch pages, sites and searches on a schedule, and get told what changed. Free to create; each check is billed per page."
openapi: "POST /monitor"
---

Creates a monitor and returns `201` with its settings and webhook secret.

**Free to create.** Each check costs 1 credit per page fetched, plus extras. See [Limits and credits](/monitors/limits). Failed requests are never charged.
