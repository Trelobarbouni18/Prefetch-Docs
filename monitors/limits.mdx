## What a check costs

Creating, reading, changing and deleting a monitor is free. **Each check** is billed to the API key that created the monitor:

| What | Credits |
|---|---|
| Each page fetched | 1, the [`/scrape`](/web-data/scrape) rate |
| A `json` target's extraction | +3, and only when the page's text changed |
| Each search a `search` target runs | 2, the [`/search`](/web-data/search) rate, +1 per new result read |
| Each page judged against your goal | 1. Only changed, new or removed pages are judged. A verdict reused from before is free. |

Pages that fail to load are never charged, and neither are skipped checks.

`estimated_credits_per_check` and `estimated_credits_per_month` on every monitor are upper bounds: they assume every page is fetched and judged on every check. `actual_credits` on a finished check is what you paid.

For example, a check of 18 pages where one page tracks `json` and changed, and 3 pages were judged, costs 18 + 3 + 3 = 24 credits.

## When credits run out

A check never spends more than its key has left. If credits run out partway, the check stops and reports `partial`. If the key has none at all, the check is recorded as `skipped_no_credits`, and the monitor keeps its schedule and resumes as soon as credits refill.

## Automatic pausing

A monitor whose every page has failed for 10 checks in a row, over at least 24 hours, is paused, and its email recipients are told. Failed pages are never billed, so this only stops it retrying a page that's gone or blocks every fetch. Set `status` back to `"active"` once the pages load.

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Targets per monitor | 50 |
| URLs per scrape target | 100 |
| Searches per search target | 5, of up to 20 results each |
| Pages per check, across all targets | 500 |

The rest depend on your plan:

| Limit | Free | Starter | Pro | Growth | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shortest schedule | Hourly | Every 5 minutes | Every 5 minutes | Every 2 minutes | Every minute |
| Monitors per API key | 5 | 50 | 250 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| Pages per check | 10 | 500 | 500 | 500 | 500 |
| Check history (`retention_days`, 30 by default) | Up to 30 days | Up to 365 | Up to 365 | Up to 365 | Up to 365 |
| Email addresses per monitor | 1, your account's | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Pages that block bots | Not retried | Retried through a stealth browser and proxies | Retried | Retried | Retried |

A request that asks for more than your plan allows is refused, and the error names the limit.

A monitor made on a bigger plan keeps running after a downgrade. A schedule that's now too frequent moves to the fastest your plan allows, and its email recipients are told once. Its history is cut to your plan's window.

A check that runs longer than 30 minutes stops and reports `partial`.
